# Community workshop pilot notes (fictional)

- The sponsor wants to reduce manual work for a community workshop booking program in Doha.
- Pilot duration is six weeks with up to 40 participants across two workshops.
- Staff currently reconcile sign-ups and reminders in spreadsheets.
- The pilot covers registration, waitlists, reminders and a weekly exception report. Keep a manual fallback.
- The trial budget ceiling is QAR 12,000. Supplier costs are still unknown.
- Confirm at least 90% of registrations within one business day and produce a weekly report that staff can reproduce.
- The sponsor needs to approve the data retention period and name the person who owns exceptions. No rollout beyond the pilot is approved.
